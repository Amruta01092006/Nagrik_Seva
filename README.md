# Nagrik_Seva
# 🌍 Civic Issue Reporting & Resolution Platform

An **AI-powered civic-tech platform** designed to bridge the gap between citizens, municipal authorities, and NGOs by making civic issue reporting faster, smarter, and more transparent.

Citizens can report problems such as **potholes, garbage accumulation, water leakage, clogged drains, damaged roads, and other civic issues** simply by uploading a photograph. The system uses AI to identify and categorize the issue, captures its location, and forwards the report to the appropriate authority or organization.

---

## 🚀 Problem Statement
A digital platform to crowdsource societal challenges and facilitate collaborative problem-solving through universities and industry partnerships

Many civic issues remain unresolved because:

* Citizens lack an easy and centralized way to report problems.
* Manual complaint registration can be time-consuming.
* Authorities may struggle to identify and prioritize issues efficiently.
* Lack of location-based information makes field verification difficult.
* Citizens often have limited visibility into the status of their complaints.

Our platform aims to create a **smart, transparent, and community-driven civic reporting system**.

---

## 💡 Proposed Solution

The platform provides a single interface where citizens can:

1. 📸 Upload an image of a civic problem.
2. 🤖 Get the issue automatically identified using AI.
3. 📍 Capture and map the location of the issue.
4. 📝 Submit the report to the appropriate authority.
5. 🔔 Track the progress of the complaint.
6. ⭐ Earn points for meaningful civic participation.
7. 🏆 Participate in a monthly leaderboard.

Municipal authorities and NGOs receive a dedicated dashboard to **view, categorize, prioritize, and manage reported issues**.
